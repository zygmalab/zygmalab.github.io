Golden rules
  1. $1,000 Digital Case Planning covers every design stage of a case, from smile design to the final zirconia file, when all stages are planned and designed by ZYGMA, in ZYGMA's system.
  2. Fabrication is always separate: guides, printed models, try-ins and PMMA / printed prototypes are billed per unit or per arch, with no additional design fee while the case stays with ZYGMA.
  3. A case that arrives with planning or stages done elsewhere is not covered by the package: ZYGMA charges $300 per stage it corrects or continues.
  4. Typical first invoice (one arch): planning $1,000 + guides + models + try-in. Later: fabrication of new PMMA or printed prototypes only. Final zirconia hybrid billed per arch at delivery review.
  5. Prices are per arch unless marked per unit or per case. A case needed in under 7 days is a rush: +20% on every item requested. Shipping and remakes caused by changes after approval are quoted separately.
